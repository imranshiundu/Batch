# Batch Product Brief

## One-line definition

Batch is a conditional commerce app where buyers commit funds into live group deals. If enough demand forms before the deadline, the batch clears, suppliers fulfill the order, and each buyer receives their own allocation. If the deal fails, funds return automatically.

## What Batch is

- A deal-clearing app
- A conditional procurement system
- A buyer commitment engine
- A supplier demand-locking tool
- An escrow and milestone release workflow

## What Batch is not

- Not a Shopify clone
- Not Jumia
- Not Amazon
- Not crowdfunding
- Not a prediction market
- Not an AI agent marketplace
- Not tokenomics

## Core user behavior

People already pool money, buy in groups, preorder goods, import together, and coordinate supplier deals in chats. Batch formalizes that behavior with deadlines, escrow, proof, allocation, and refunds.

## First wedge

Small merchant inventory batches.

Focus on goods where waiting creates a real advantage:

- phone accessories
- beauty products
- electronics accessories
- school supplies
- spare parts
- tools
- uniforms

Do not start with every category. Do not start with urgent purchases. Do not start with high-risk perishables.

## Core loop

1. Supplier creates a batch.
2. Buyers commit funds to units.
3. The batch clears if the threshold is reached before the deadline.
4. Supplier confirms and fulfills through milestones.
5. Buyers receive individual allocations.
6. Supplier is paid by proof-based release.
7. Failed batches refund automatically.

## Why buyers wait

- Strong price advantage
- Visible progress
- Fixed deadline
- Escrow protection
- Delivery tracking
- Refund rules
- Slot transfer after clearing

## Why suppliers wait

- Confirmed demand
- Bulk order certainty
- Lower sales risk
- Staged payouts
- Repeat buyer groups
- Reputation from successful delivery

## Arc and Circle alignment

Batch creates non-speculative stablecoin payment flows: commitments, escrow funding, refunds, supplier payouts, milestone releases, cross-border settlement, logistics payments, and recurring merchant restock cycles.

Circle and Arc benefit when Batch turns stablecoin rails into real commerce infrastructure instead of trading speculation.
